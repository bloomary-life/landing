AI Catalyst application

Bloomary helps people become more visible without losing themselves to AI slop.

Bloomary is an AI-assisted writing and publishing product for people who freeze in front of an empty text field, judge their ideas too early, and still want to show their work publicly in their own voice.

Open web app Product website

Use case

From private thought to public professional identity.

The problem

Many professionals have useful ideas, hard-earned lessons, and lived expertise, but the blank page turns visibility into anxiety. AI adds another fear: devaluation, the perception of self-unworthiness, and the sense that human voice will become less valuable, replaceable, or economically unsafe.

The Bloomary answer

Bloomary uses AI as a supportive editor, not a replacement author. It helps users shape rough thoughts into LinkedIn-ready drafts, preserve their own point of view, and publish before self-judgment kills the idea.

What exists now

A working product across mobile, web, backend, and deployment that became possible thanks to AI.

01

Native iOS app

Swift app with editor, LinkedIn-style preview, drafts, LinkedIn OAuth, publishing, feedback, account settings, logout, and account/data deletion. The iOS app is awaiting Apple App Review.

02

Native Android app

Kotlin Android implementation for the same writing flow: drafts, editor, account state, reminders, entitlement-aware behavior, and LinkedIn publishing workflows. The Android app is awaiting Google Play readiness, including D-U-N-S and related setup.

03

Web app

Browser-based Bloomary experience available at platform.bloomary.life, with the public product and pricing pages available at bloomary.life.

04

Go backend

API backend for LinkedIn auth and publishing, draft sync, account deletion, feedback email, entitlement checks, telemetry, rate limits, and operational safety.

05

Deployment automation

Ansible and deploy scripts for stage and production environments, including backend, landing, platform, server setup, security setup, and database backup paths.

06

App Review media

Remotion-based app preview planning and screen-recording scenarios for explaining the product flow clearly in App Store review and marketing materials.

07

Job-graph descriptions

Job-graph descriptions that map the emotional and practical steps from a private idea to a visible public post: fear, self-judgment, draft shaping, feedback, publishing, and confidence.

How AI is used

AI supports the human, the product, and the go-to-market system.

In the product Voice-aware editing, idea expansion, critique, rewrite suggestions, and post-shaping without turning the user into a generic AI persona.

In research Evidence-based synthetic ICP research: 200 personas built from real competitor signals, reviews, comments, and job-to-be-done patterns.

In positioning AI-assisted competitor analysis, pricing research, landing-page messaging, and validation of mobile-first LinkedIn consistency pains.

In operations AI-assisted implementation reviews, App Review preparation, support copy, telemetry planning, and release-readiness checks.

In content workflows Video and social-content workflows that turn product footage, scripts, and product thinking into clearer public artifacts.

Why it matters

The emotional job is bigger than posting.

People fear becoming visible.

The market is full of anxiety that AI will replace human work. People also fear becoming visible: showing their ideas, being judged, and discovering whether their experience is valuable. Bloomary is built from the opposite belief: AI should help people reveal their explorations, taste, story, and professional self more clearly.

The founder mission is personal.

Helping other people become more fully themselves is also my own dream. I myself went through self-devaluing my experience, feeling unworthy and helpless. I wish to help others go through these invisible, illusory borders. Bloomary is designed to gently nourish ideas until users can publish consistently and move toward the professional identity they have always wanted to grow into.
