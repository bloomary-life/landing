FAQ

What does the free trial include?

Every Pro feature for 14 days: the editor, AI tools, images, publishing and scheduling to LinkedIn, and 50 drafts synced across your devices. One trial per person — deleting your account and signing up again does not start a new one. Bloomary never adds a signature to your posts.

What does Pro add?

Pro keeps everything from the trial after it ends: publishing, scheduling posts up to 31 days ahead, editing and deleting drafts, 50 drafts with cross-device sync, and AI clients connected through MCP.

Why are there two price tiers?

See pricing on our Paddle page.

Is there a yearly plan?

Available billing options and regional pricing are shown at checkout.

What happens to my drafts when my trial or Pro ends?

Your drafts are never deleted. They become read-only: you can read, copy, and export them on any of your devices, and save new drafts until you have 5 in total. Editing, deleting, publishing, and scheduling need an active subscription. Posts you already scheduled still publish on time. You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

Do you refund paid subscriptions?

Submit refund requests within 14 days of the transaction date. Paddle reviews requests under its Refund Policy.

Does Bloomary include AI writing?

Bloomary uses BYOK AI for grammar help and Make it natural rewriting. You provide and control your own AI API key. Bloomary does not generate posts for you — it helps you write better posts yourself.

Which platforms are available?

Bloomary is available on web (platform.bloomary.life) and iOS (TestFlight, App Store coming soon). Android is planned.

Billing

The free trial requires no payment. Bloomary Pro is billed monthly or yearly. Web subscriptions may be processed by Paddle as merchant of record and reseller. iOS subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Cancellation

You can cancel future renewal at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal and does not automatically refund the current billing period.

After Your Trial or Subscription Ends

Your drafts stay available read-only on all your devices: you can read, copy, and export them as a zip of Markdown files. You can save new drafts until you have 5 in total; saved drafts cannot be edited or deleted until you subscribe. Publishing and scheduling need an active subscription; posts scheduled earlier still publish.
