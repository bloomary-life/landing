Account Deletion

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Bloomary is operated by DailyMind LTD. This page explains how to delete your Bloomary account data and what happens after deletion.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, Gemini API keys, payment card details, or client secrets in an account deletion request.

Delete From The App

If you can access the Bloomary app, open Account and use Delete all my data. The app asks for confirmation before deletion.

This removes the stored LinkedIn connection on the backend where it exists, clears local app tokens and drafts where the app can do so, clears editor state, and returns the app to the signed-out state.

Request Deletion By Email

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access it, email info@dailymind.eu with the subject Bloomary account deletion request.

Please include enough information to identify the account, such as:

  • the email address used with LinkedIn sign-in, if known;
  • your LinkedIn profile URL, if you connected LinkedIn;
  • the platform you used: iOS, Android, or web.

We may ask for additional confirmation if needed to prevent deleting the wrong account. We normally complete valid deletion requests within 30 days.

Data Deleted

  • Bloomary LinkedIn auth/profile records linked to the identified account.
  • Backend per-day posting/request counts linked to the identified LinkedIn member ID.
  • Local drafts, tokens, and app state where deletion is performed inside the app.

Data You May Need To Delete Locally

DailyMind LTD cannot directly erase data stored only on your own device or browser after the app is removed. You can also clear Bloomary local data by deleting the app, clearing browser site data for bloomary.life and platform.bloomary.life, or clearing app storage on your device.

Data We May Retain

We may retain limited records where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, dispute, or support reasons. Feedback messages may be retained for up to 12 months unless a longer period is needed for those reasons.
